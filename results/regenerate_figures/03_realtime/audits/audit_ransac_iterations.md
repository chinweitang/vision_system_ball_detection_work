### What `wall_ms` actually times

Quoted from the producer, not paraphrased:

```python
t0 = time.perf_counter()
            try:
                res = ransac_fit(win["t_win"], win["xyz_win"], fit_fn, predict_fn,
                                  min_samples=MIN_SAMPLES_C, inlier_threshold_mm=RANSAC_INLIER_THRESHOLD_MM,
                                  n_iterations=n_iter, random_seed=seed, frame_numbers=win["frame_win"])
                wall_ms = (time.perf_counter() - t0) * 1000.0
```

So `wall_ms` brackets **the `ransac_fit` call alone** - one model, one RANSAC
fit. It excludes detection, pairing, triangulation and every other stage. The
producer states this is deliberate: the per-flight track and target are built
once per flight, and only the RANSAC call is re-run across the grid.
