### Observation window / points fitted

There is **no observation-window sweep in Stage 1**. Each flight is fitted once
on its whole usable track:

- points fitted per flight (`single_shot_fit_n`): [15, 19, 44, 54, 72, 81, 87, 89]
- pairs available (`n_pairs`): [15, 19, 44, 54, 72, 81, 87, 89]

So the x-quantity is a **point count**, not a time window. That is the key
difference from the later sweep, which sweeps 150–1250 ms.
