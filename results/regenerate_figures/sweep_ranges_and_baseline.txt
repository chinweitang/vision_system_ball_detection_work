2c. Across all 163 flights - AND THE WEIGHTING QUESTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT: no 163-flight figure is STORED anywhere. Each CSV carries only
its own session AVERAGE. The combined figure must be computed, and the
answer depends on the weighting, so both are given:

  FLIGHT-UNWEIGHTED mean across 163 flights = 0.161643  (16.16%)
      = mean of the 163 individual per-flight combined_rate values,
        each flight weighted equally.

  POOLED FRAME-LEVEL rate across 163 flights = 1252 / 11052 = 0.113283 (11.33%)
      = sum(combined_detections) / sum(combined_processable),
        i.e. weighted by each flight's number of co-processable frames.
      Per session, pooled: 07_15 = 293/2665 = 0.109944
                           07_21 = 959/8387 = 0.114344

ANSWER TO THE WEIGHTING QUESTION:
  The figure that matches this codebase's own convention is the
  FLIGHT-UNWEIGHTED MEAN = 0.161643. It is NOT a pooled frame-level rate.
  Evidence from 05_detection_rate_summary.py:
    l.132-133  valid_rates = [per-flight combined_rate ...]
               avg_combined_rate = sum(valid_rates) / len(valid_rates)
  i.e. a simple mean over flights, each flight weighted equally
  regardless of how many frames it contains. The module header states the
  same: "simple mean of combined_rate across flights, each flight
  weighted equally".

  The gap between the two (16.16% vs 11.33%) is large and not incidental:
  the unweighted mean is pulled up by short flights with few processable
  frames and high rates (e.g. 07_15 flight_01: 24/25 = 0.96 over just 25
  frames), which the pooled rate down-weights. Quote whichever is intended,
  but do not present 16.16% as a frame-level detection rate.

CAVEAT ON A THIRD, WRONG NUMBER: averaging the two session AVERAGEs
directly gives (0.1978 + 0.1510)/2 = 0.1744 (17.44%). That is a
SESSION-unweighted mean and matches neither figure above - it ignores
that 07_21 has 126 flights against 07_15's 37. Do not use it.

NOTE ON PRECISION: per-flight combined_rate is stored rounded to 4 dp, so
the unweighted mean 0.161643 inherits that rounding; recomputing it from
the raw combined_detections/combined_processable integers per flight would
shift it only in the 5th decimal. The pooled figure is exact (integer
counts).
